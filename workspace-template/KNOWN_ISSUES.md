# Known Issues and Practice Adaptations

Register every adaptation of the deployed `_practice` copy here at the moment of change. The practice lead harvests this file at each update, adopted improvements come back in the next practice release, and anything not adopted stays available in the update snapshot.

Format, one entry per change:

## KI-001 <short title>

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- File changed: _practice\<path>
- What changed: <one or two sentences>
- Why: <the gap in the practice this works around>
- Status: Open | Harvested | Adopted in vX.Y.Z | Not adopted, kept local
